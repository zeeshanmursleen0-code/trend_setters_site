INDEPENDENT PERSONAL PROJECT

Affiliate Product Research and Content Preparation

Trend Setters is a personal Python project that collects, organizes, and ranks product information obtained through an affiliate-program API. It helps its operator compare products and prepare records for potential content workflows.

Explore the project

About Trend Setters

The purpose of Trend Setters is to make affiliate product research more organized. The current application runs locally on the operator's computer and saves product information in CSV files for review.

Product Collection

Retrieve available product titles, prices, images, product URLs, and commission information from the affiliate API.

Product Ranking

Compare available sales-volume indicators, estimated commission, evaluation data, and discounts to prioritize products for further review.

Content Preparation

Organize product details and affiliate URLs for possible future content workflows. Product data can change and should be checked before publication.

Current Functionality

The current version collects and ranks affiliate product information locally and exports the results to CSV files.

Pinterest publishing is not connected or active. This website does not currently publish Pins, access Pinterest user accounts, or claim that automated publishing is operational.

Any future integration will be developed using the relevant authorized API and permissions.

Affiliate Disclosure

Some links prepared by this project may be affiliate links. If a qualifying purchase is made through an affiliate link, the operator may earn a commission at no additional cost to the buyer. Commission eligibility and rates depend on the relevant affiliate program.

Product rankings are based on available data and do not guarantee product quality, sales, conversions, or earnings. Prices, discounts, availability, and commission rates may change.
