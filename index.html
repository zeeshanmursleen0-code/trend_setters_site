Trend Setters

Pinterest Affiliate Marketing Automation

About the project

Trend Setters is a personal software project being developed to help its operator prepare product information and manage product Pins for affiliate marketing.

Planned functions may include organizing product names, affiliate destination links, image URLs, board identifiers, and Pin descriptions, and publishing Pins through Pinterest's authorized API when access is available.

How it works

The tool is intended to process product details supplied by its operator. Any Pinterest publishing will require account authorization and the permissions approved by Pinterest. It is not an official Pinterest product and is not endorsed by Pinterest.

Privacy

Read the Trend Setters Privacy Policy for information about data handling.

Affiliate disclosure

Product Pins created with this tool may contain affiliate links. The operator may earn a commission when a user makes a qualifying purchase through such a link, at no additional cost to the purchaser where applicable.
